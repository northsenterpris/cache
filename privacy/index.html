Privacy Policy

Cache: Private Expense Tracker · Effective October 22, 2026

In short: everything you log in Cache stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no login and no bank link. Cache sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, which you can switch off at any time. There are no ads, no tracking and we never sell data.

Your expenses stay on your device

Expenses, amounts, notes, dates, budgets, custom category names, your currency and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside Cache's own app storage (shared with the Cache widget on the same device). We have no server that stores this data, and we cannot see it. Cache has no account and no sign-in.

If you export a CSV file or PDF report, the file is created on your iPhone and only goes where you send it with the share sheet.

Anonymous usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which features help people and where the app can improve, Cache uses Google Analytics for Firebase. When this is on, the app sends:

DataExamples
Product interactionAn expense was logged, a budget was set, the paywall was shown, a chart was viewed, onboarding was finished.
Other usage dataRough ranges instead of values (for example "an amount between $10 and $50", "hours since install: 1–24"), whether you use Pro, a budget, a reminder or an alternate app icon, and Firebase's standard session events. When you buy Cache Pro, Firebase is told which product was bought and its price, so purchases can be counted.
Device identifierA random app-instance ID created by Firebase, and Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV). These are not linked to your name or any account. Cache does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Cache never sends amounts, notes or the names of categories you create. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. Analytics data is kept for at most 2 months at the user level, then only in aggregate reports.

Turning it off: switch off "Share anonymous usage data" in onboarding, or at any time in Cache › Settings › Privacy. Collection stops immediately, and nothing more is sent until you switch it back on.

Purchases

Cache Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and Cache never sees your name, email, card or other payment details. The app only receives the confirmation from Apple that you are entitled to Pro. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.

What Cache does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Cache never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No access to your bank, contacts, photos or location.

Notifications and Face ID

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your iPhone; no server is involved. If you turn on Face ID lock, authentication is handled by iOS. Cache never receives your face data or passcode.

Deleting your data

To delete everything Cache has stored, delete the app (and its widget) from your iPhone. Because there is no account or server copy, that removes all of your data. Anonymous analytics data cannot be linked back to you, so it cannot be looked up or deleted individually; it expires as described above.

Children

Cache is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the App Store release notes.
